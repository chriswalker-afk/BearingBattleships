📐 Bearings maths help

These examples explain the skill without giving away where any creature is.

What is a bearing?

A bearing is an angle measured clockwise from north, written using three figures. For example, east is 090°, south is 180°, west is 270° and north is 000°.

How do I use the protractor?
  1. Drag its centre onto your ship.
  2. Rotate it so its 0° / N direction points upwards (north).
  3. Read clockwise from north to the creature, using its position on the circular scale.
  4. Type a three-figure bearing in Captain's orders.

Think of 045° as halfway between north and east, while 315° is northwest.

How do I use the ruler?

The ruler pivots around the blue 0 mark at the centre of its short end, not its middle. Drag its body to position that mark on your ship. Then drag the orange handle on its far end to turn the ruler towards a creature without shifting the 0 mark. Read distance from 0 along the marked edge; each grid square is 1 unit. Bubble range is up to 8 units; movement is limited to 1–5 units.

Why do turns alternate between Fire and Move?

Each valid turn has a set phase: Fire → Move → Fire → Move. You start by firing. After you complete the order, surviving sea sillies swim closer and the next phase is announced. The highlighted phase and large action button show what you must do now. You cannot skip a phase. This continues across levels, including two-hit helmet enemies.

An invalid bearing, distance or off-map move does not use a turn or change your phase.

What does an incorrect bearing warning mean?

Use a three-digit bearing from 000° to 359° (e.g. 005°, 045°, 270°). North is 000°, not 360°. The warning pop-in explains whether digits are missing or the number is out of range. Correct the entry and try again: your turn is not lost.

How do three-figure bearings work?

Always write three digits: 5° is 005°, 45° is 045°, and 120° remains 120°. 360° points north too, but this game uses 000°.

What if I measure an angle anticlockwise?

Bearings still go clockwise. If a direction is 40° anticlockwise from north, its bearing is 360° − 40° = 320°.

Worked example: firing northeast

A target is 3 squares east and 3 squares north of your ship. It is halfway between north and east: the bearing is 045°. Its distance is about 4.2 units, so a bubble with range 5 units reaches it. This is a generic example, not a game hint.

What changes as I reach higher levels?

There are 12 levels. Each new level adds only one challenge: one extra creature, a small increase in how far they swim per turn, or one additional creature wearing a bubble helmet. Other rules remain the same.

From Level 4, some sea sillies have two-hit helmets. Their blue ring and small number show how many hits remain. Aim at the centre of the creature, not the number badge. One hit cracks the helmet without scoring a point; the next accurate hit pops the creature. Your pop streak is not lost when you hit a helmet.

How do I collect bonus stars?

When you pop a sea silly, it releases a glowing ⭐ bonus star. It floats closer to your ship so you can normally reach it on your next MOVE PHASE. Sail through or close to the star to collect 2 extra points. Your ruler and protractor are still needed to choose the bearing and distance. Stars remain available for four moving opportunities, then drift away. A first hit on a two-hit helmet is not a pop, so it does not drop a star.

All enemies begin within your eight-unit firing range. If sailing would leave every enemy too far away, the nearest silly catches a current and swims back into range before your next firing turn.

Why might a shot miss?

Check that your protractor is centred on the ship, 0° points north, you have measured clockwise, and your range is long enough. A bubble travels in a straight line along the bearing you enter.

Challenge: reverse bearings

The bearing from A to B is 070°. From B to A, turn 180°: the reverse bearing is 250°. If the original bearing is over 180°, subtract 180° instead.
